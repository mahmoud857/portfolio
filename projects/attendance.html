Fingerprint Attendance Dashboard

A dashboard that finds missing fingerprint punches across a group of factories, so managers can evaluate each branch and see which shift and supervisor the problem comes from.

RoleData analyst, end to end
Data sourcesCompany ERP and a second platform
ScopeMultiple factories
ToolsExcel, XLOOKUP, IFS, Power Query

Project goal

The company wanted to evaluate the performance of every branch (factory) month by month, based on attendance fingerprint problems.

The dashboard gives the manager three answers: what type of problem happened, in which shift it happened, and under which supervisor. With this, the manager can follow up on the right people instead of reading thousands of records.

The data

I used two sources, not only the attendance sheet:

  • ERP attendance export: attendance of employees from a group of factories, with names, national IDs, department, job title and more.
  • A second platform: employee data, attendance, entry and exit times, total working hours and other fields.

Problems in the data

The raw data was not ready for analysis. It had:

  • Many empty (missing) values
  • Numbers that were incorrect or inconsistent
  • Columns with no defined data type

Attendance status types

Every record was classified into one status. My main focus was on the three fingerprint problems: IN, OUT and IN/OUT.

StatusMeaning
DoneNo problem. The employee punched in and out.
INProblem: the employee did not punch in.
OUTProblem: the employee did not punch out.
IN/OUTProblem: the employee did not punch in or out.
N-U-SNot uploaded on the system.

What I did

  1. Exported the attendance data from the ERP system for all factories.
  2. Exported a second sheet from another platform with employee data, entry and exit times and total working hours.
  3. Set the data type for every column (text, number, date, time) so the data behaves correctly in formulas.
  4. Used XLOOKUP formulas to bring the missing information from the second sheet into the main sheet.
  5. Determined the problem type of each record with formulas (IFS), and gave it one of the statuses above.
  6. Built the dashboard to show the problems by status, branch, shift and job.

What the dashboard shows

  • KPI cards for each status, total attendance and number of problems
  • Problem status as a share of all records
  • Total issues by branch for each day
  • Problems by shift and supervisor
  • Problems by job

Key figuresFrom the final dashboard

29,235Total attendance records
19,386Done (no problem)
9,770IN, OUT and IN/OUT problems
8,534IN/OUT (no punch at all)

What the analysis showed

Most of the problems were IN/OUT, which means employees with no punch at all. It was about 87% of all the problems found. Knowing this lets the manager focus on one clear issue, and on the branches, shifts and supervisors where it happens most.

This project uses real company data. Names, national IDs and company details are masked or hidden in the screenshots, and the original files are not published to protect employee privacy.
