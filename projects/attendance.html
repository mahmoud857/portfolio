Fingerprint Attendance Dashboard

Turning raw fingerprint machine exports from several factories into one clear view of attendance problems.

RoleData analyst, end to end
ContextReal company data, anonymized
ToolsExcel, Power Query

The problem

Each factory exported fingerprint logs separately. Finding late arrivals, missing punches and recurring problems meant reading long sheets by hand, and managers could not compare factories or shifts.

What I did

  • Combined the exports from all factories into one clean table with Power Query
  • Cleaned duplicate and incomplete punches and standardized shifts, positions and dates
  • Defined attendance problem types and a status for each case
  • Built the dashboard on top of the cleaned table

What the dashboard shows

  • Attendance problems by factory, shift, position and month
  • The status of each problem, so open cases are easy to follow
  • Filters that let a manager focus on one factory or period

Impact

Managers get one place to see where attendance problems happen and when.

This project uses data from a company I work with. Names, IDs and company details are hidden or masked in the screenshots, and the original files are not published to protect employee privacy.
