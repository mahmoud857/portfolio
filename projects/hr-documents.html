HR Document Tracking System

An Excel system that tracks employee documents and shows what is missing.

RoleAnalysis, build and design
ToolsExcel, XLOOKUP

The problem

Employee documents were tracked in separate lists. HR could not easily see which documents were missing or how complete each factory was.

What I did

  • Organized all employee document records in one table
  • Used XLOOKUP to match employees with their documents
  • Added data validation to keep entries consistent
  • Calculated document completion for each factory

What it delivers

  • A list of missing documents per employee
  • Completion rate across factories

Impact

HR sees missing documents immediately instead of searching lists.

Company details and employee data are hidden or not published to protect privacy.
