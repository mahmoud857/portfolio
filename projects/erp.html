ERP Dashboard Redesign

A new home screen for a company ERP, designed for Arabic right-to-left use.

RoleAnalysis, build and design
ToolsHTML, CSS, UI design

The problem

The ERP home screen was hard to scan. Sections were not clearly separated, and the colors and font made it tiring to use every day.

What I did

  • Redesigned the home screen as a right-to-left Arabic layout
  • Created one card for each ERP section, plus a sidebar for navigation
  • Chose a new color palette and a more readable font

What it delivers

  • A clear entry point to every department
  • Consistent cards, spacing and typography

Impact

Users reach any section faster from one clean screen.

This is a design project. Company details and employee data are hidden or not published to protect privacy.
