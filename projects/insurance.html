Employee Insurance Coverage Dashboard

An Excel dashboard that shows who is covered by insurance and who is not, across locations and jobs.

RoleData analyst, end to end
ContextReal company data, anonymized
ToolsExcel

The problem

Insurance coverage was tracked in lists that were hard to summarize. HR could not quickly see how many employees were covered, where the gaps were, or which groups needed follow-up.

What I did

  • Organized the employee insurance records into one structured table
  • Defined the KPIs for coverage and non-coverage
  • Built charts by location, job, gender and status
  • Refined the colors and layout so the KPIs read at a glance

What the dashboard shows

  • KPI cards for the overall coverage picture
  • Coverage split by location, job, gender and status

Impact

HR can answer coverage questions in seconds instead of counting rows.

This project uses data from a company I work with. Names, IDs and company details are hidden or masked in the screenshots, and the original files are not published to protect employee privacy.
